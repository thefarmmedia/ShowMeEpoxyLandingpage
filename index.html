Straight Answers

Before You Book.

How long does installation take?

Many standard garage systems can be installed in one day. You can typically walk on the floor after 24 hours and drive on it after 48 hours.

Why does diamond grinding matter?

Grinding opens the concrete pores and creates the surface profile needed for a strong mechanical bond. It is one of the most important steps in preventing peeling.

Is the calculator a final quote?

No. It gives you a useful ballpark based on your selections. Final pricing is confirmed after the space is measured and the slab is inspected.

Can I see color samples in person?

Yes. The visualizer helps narrow your choices, and physical samples are brought to your free on-site quote so you can see them in your actual lighting.

What kinds of spaces do you coat?

Show Me Epoxy coats garages, basements, patios, shops, commercial floors, and other concrete spaces throughout Mid-Missouri.
